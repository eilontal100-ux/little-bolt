## Concrete direction for our original artwork
These are design conclusions from the references, not claims of implemented work.
- Draw recognizable original human or creature characters with a deliberate silhouette, face, clothing and distinct animation poses on a consistent pixel grid.
- Compose material-specific tiles with coherent light/shadow clusters; avoid random rectangles and thick featureless blocks.
- Foreground collision edges must be clear. Keep decorative backgrounds lower contrast, with architectural landmarks and purposeful props.
- Prefer a fresh setting such as a sunlit desert bazaar and buried palace, rooftop harbor adventure, or haunted clockwork city. No robot/forest reskin.
- Build rooms around a verb: bounce, climb, activate, evade, rescue. Introduce a mechanic safely, vary it, then combine it with another.
- Longer levels need branching secret rooms, visible locked objectives, shortcuts and checkpoints, not stretched corridors or repeated gaps.
- Render authored pixel art crisply. Lowering the canvas resolution alone is not the art direction.

Ava's five visual concepts remain in progress. Apply these principles to those concepts and then integrate the selected direction into actual game code. Reference screenshots and game characters are not copied into the shipped project.
